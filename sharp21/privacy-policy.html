Privacy Policy

Sharp 21 — Blackjack Strategy Trainer • Last updated: March 18, 2026

The short version: Sharp 21 does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything stays on your device. We have no servers, no accounts, and no analytics.

Data Collection

Sharp 21 does not collect any personal information. Specifically:

  • No account creation or login is required
  • No personal data (name, email, phone number, location) is collected
  • No usage analytics or tracking is performed
  • No cookies or tracking pixels are used
  • No data is transmitted to any server
  • No third-party analytics SDKs are included

Local Data Storage

Sharp 21 stores your game progress, settings, and session history locally on your device using on-device storage (MMKV). This data includes:

  • Your rule set preferences and counting configuration
  • Session history and accuracy statistics
  • Mistake journal entries
  • Sharp rating and best run scores
  • Bet spread configuration

This data never leaves your device. It is not backed up to any cloud service by the app. If you delete the app, all locally stored data is permanently removed.

In-App Purchases

Sharp 21 offers an optional one-time in-app purchase to unlock premium modes. This transaction is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not process any payment information directly. Apple's privacy policy governs the purchase transaction.

Third-Party Services

Sharp 21 does not integrate with any third-party services that collect user data. The app functions entirely offline with no network requirement.

Children's Privacy

Sharp 21 does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The app contains no gambling with real money — it is a strategy training tool only.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will post the revised version on this page with an updated date. Since no data is collected, changes would only reflect new features or clarifications.
