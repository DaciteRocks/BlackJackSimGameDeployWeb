Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the app, go to Settings, and tap "Restore Purchases." This will restore your premium access if you've previously purchased it on the same Apple ID.

Is an internet connection required?

No. Blackjack Sharp Trainer works entirely offline. No account, no login, no internet needed. The only exception is the initial in-app purchase, which requires a connection to the App Store.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?

Yes. All data (session history, mistake journal, settings) is stored locally on your device. Deleting the app removes all data permanently.

What rule sets are supported?

Blackjack Sharp Trainer supports H17/S17, DAS, RSA, late/early/no surrender, 3:2 and 6:5 blackjack payouts, and 1/2/4/6/8 deck shoes. The strategy engine adjusts correct plays based on your selected rules.

What counting system does the app use?

The card counting simulator uses Hi-Lo, the most widely used counting system. Additional systems may be added in future updates.

Does The Sharp mode require card counting knowledge?

No. The Sharp is a roguelite mode based entirely on basic strategy decisions. No counting required — it's accessible to all players.

Is this real gambling?

No. Blackjack Sharp Trainer is a strategy training tool. There is no real money involved in gameplay. All chips and scores are simulated for educational purposes only.
