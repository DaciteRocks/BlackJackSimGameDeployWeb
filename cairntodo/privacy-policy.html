Cairn: To Do — Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 14, 2026

Overview

Cairn: To Do ("Cairn," "the app," "we") is a personal task management app. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Data Storage

All tasks, lists, settings, and preferences you create in Cairn are stored locally on your device only. Your data never leaves your phone. There are no user accounts, no cloud sync, and no remote servers.

Data Collection

Cairn does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Specifically:

  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising or ad identifiers
  • No personal information is collected (name, email, location, etc.)
  • No data is sent to any server, third party, or external service

Calendar Access

Cairn offers an optional feature to import events from your device's calendar as tasks. If you enable this feature, the app reads calendar event data (titles, dates, times, locations, and notes) from calendars you explicitly select. This data is read locally on your device and stored as tasks within the app. Calendar data is never transmitted off your device.

You can disable calendar access at any time in the app's settings or in your device's system settings.

Notifications

Cairn may request permission to send local push notifications for task reminders. These notifications are generated entirely on your device. No notification data is sent to any server.

Third-Party Services

Cairn does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or data processing services.

Children's Privacy

Cairn does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
