Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?

All your tasks, lists, and settings are stored locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded to the cloud or any external server.

Will I lose my data if I delete the app?

Yes. Since all data is stored on your device, uninstalling the app will remove your tasks and settings. We recommend completing or noting important tasks before uninstalling.

How do recurring tasks work?

When you complete a recurring task, the app automatically creates the next occurrence based on your repeat schedule. The original completed task stays in your history.

What is auto-rollover?

Tasks with auto-rollover enabled will automatically move to today if they weren't completed by their due date, so nothing falls through the cracks.

How do I change the app theme?

Go to Settings and choose from six color themes: Teal, Ocean, Lavender, Rose, Sunset, or Forest. Dark mode follows your device's system setting.
